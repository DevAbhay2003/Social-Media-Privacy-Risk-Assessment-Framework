🔐 Core Cybersecurity & Privacy Fundamentals

Mastering the conceptual distinctions between account security and information privacy.

Privacy vs. Security

Privacy: Controls how personal information is collected, exposed, shared, and utilized.
Security: Protects systems, accounts, and credentials from unauthorized access or compromise.

💡 Key Insight: A user may have a 30-character random password and a FIDO2 hardware key (Strong Security), but still post their direct phone number, birth date, and live GPS check-ins publicly (Weak Privacy). Strong Account Security ≠ Strong Privacy.

Digital Footprint & Oversharing

Active Footprint: Information deliberately broadcasted (posts, photos, tagged relations, resumes).
Passive Footprint: Interaction trails and metadata harvested by platforms and aggregators.

🎯 Social Engineering Link: Threat actors aggregate open-source intelligence (OSINT) from public posts (employer, college, vacation schedules) to craft highly convincing pretexting lures, spear-phishing emails, or emergency impersonation scams.

📍 Location Exposure

Real-time check-ins and geotagging alert adversaries to unoccupied residences, daily commute routes, and physical habits.

📜 Historical Posts

Old posts from past years often contain abandoned email addresses, maiden names, or context exploited for security question bypass.

🔑 MFA & Account Defense

Multi-Factor Authentication stops up to 99% of automated credential stuffing. App-based TOTP or hardware keys protect private messages.
