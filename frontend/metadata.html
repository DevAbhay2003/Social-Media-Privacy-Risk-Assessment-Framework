💡 What Can Photos Unintentionally Leak?

Visual context and binary metadata hazards in modern photography.

  • 📍 Exact Geolocation: Cameras and phones embed precise latitude and longitude coordinates into the EXIF header.
  • 🕒 Temporal Patterns: Timestamps reveal exact routines, arrival times, and unoccupied home intervals.
  • 🏢 Physical Badges & Lanyards: Access cards in work selfies enable barcode replication and badge cloning.
  • 🖥️ Computer Monitors & Desks: Background screens often expose internal Slack channels, dashboards, and credentials.
  • 🚗 Vehicles & House Numbers: Street signs and license plates permit automated address geolocation via Street View.
